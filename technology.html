Purpose-built AI for the world's most variable, unpredictable, and high-stakes environments.

Why We Needed to Build from Scratch

Most agricultural AI tools retrofit consumer AI onto farming problems. VitaSynora was built ground-up for the unique requirements of smallholder agriculture in emerging markets — irregular sensor connectivity, low-bandwidth environments, massive variability in soil, weather, and crop types, and the absolute intolerance for false negatives that could destroy a family's income. Our architecture is different by design.

The Data Foundation

What We Ingest

  • Multi-spectral satellite imagery (10m resolution, daily refresh)
  • IoT soil sensors: NPK, moisture, pH, temperature at 15cm intervals
  • Hyper-local weather stations: temperature, humidity, wind, rainfall
  • Drone visual feeds: RGB + Near-Infrared + Thermal imaging
  • Historical yield and input records (digitized and validated)
  • Market price data feeds from 40+ commodity markets

What We Predict

  • Disease onset 14 days before visible symptoms
  • Yield per hectare with 90%+ confidence, 6-8 weeks pre-harvest
  • Optimal irrigation timing and volume, updated every 4 hours
  • Pest migration vectors and arrival probability
  • Optimal harvest window within ±2 days
  • Commodity price movements 4-6 weeks forward

Our Model Stack

  • ResNet-50 CNNs for plant disease classification
  • LSTM time-series networks for yield prediction
  • Multi-sensor fusion algorithms for irrigation optimization
  • Transformer-based NLP models for market signal extraction
  • Anomaly detection models for field health surveillance
  • Federated learning protocols preserving farm data privacy

How We Use Amazon Web Services

  • Amazon ECS (Fargate): Containerized API services with zero-management scaling — from 10 to 10 million concurrent farm queries without manual intervention
  • Amazon S3: Petabyte-scale storage for satellite imagery, drone footage, and historical sensor logs — with lifecycle policies optimized for cost
  • Amazon RDS PostgreSQL: Managed relational database for farm profiles, field boundaries, yield records, and user data — multi-AZ for 99.99% uptime
  • Amazon SageMaker: Managed ML training pipeline — weekly model retraining on incoming field data, automated A/B evaluation, and blue-green deployment
  • Amazon Bedrock: Foundation model access for natural language advisory generation — turning AI predictions into plain-language farmer recommendations in any language
  • AWS Lambda: Event-driven serverless functions for real-time disease alerts, irrigation triggers, and market price notifications — sub-second response time
  • Amazon CloudFront: Global CDN ensuring the VitaSynora mobile app and dashboard load in under 2 seconds from Lagos to Jakarta
  • Amazon CloudWatch: End-to-end observability — model accuracy drift detection, API latency monitoring, and automated incident response

How We Use NVIDIA Technology

  • CUDA GPU Computing: All deep learning training and batch inference runs on NVIDIA GPU clusters — enabling training runs that would take weeks on CPU to complete in hours. Our disease detection model processes 50,000 images per hour on a single A100 GPU.
  • TensorRT Inference Optimization: Production inference is optimized with TensorRT — reducing model size by 4x and inference latency by 3x vs standard PyTorch deployment. This enables real-time disease detection on satellite tiles as they stream in.
  • NVIDIA Jetson Edge Modules: Jetson Nano and Orin modules deployed inside field-operated drones enable on-device inference — detecting disease from 50-meter altitude without sending raw imagery to the cloud. Zero latency. Zero connectivity required.
  • Tритоn Inference Server: Multi-model serving in production — simultaneously running crop disease classification, yield prediction, irrigation optimization, and market intelligence models with hardware-level resource sharing and priority queuing.
  • RAPIDS cuDF & cuML: GPU-accelerated data preprocessing transforms satellite imagery analysis from a 4-hour batch job to a 12-minute pipeline. This means farmers get daily field assessments, not weekly ones.

Privacy & Security

Farm data is as sensitive as financial data. VitaSynora is built with data sovereignty as a first principle.

  • ✓ Federated model training: Models improve without raw farm data leaving the device
  • ✓ End-to-end encryption: All sensor data in transit and at rest
  • ✓ Farmer ownership: Every data record is owned by the farmer who generated it
  • ✓ GDPR-aligned architecture: Full deletion rights, data minimization, audit trails
  • ✓ No third-party data sharing without explicit consent
